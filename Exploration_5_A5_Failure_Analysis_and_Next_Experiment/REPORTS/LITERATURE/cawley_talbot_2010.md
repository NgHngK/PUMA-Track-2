# On Over-fitting in Model Selection and Subsequent Selection Bias in Performance Evaluation

Gavin C. Cawley and Nicola L. C. Talbot, JMLR11,2079–2107,2010.
Primary source: https://www.jmlr.org/beta/papers/v11/cawley10a.html
Verified by web search after the local root-cause diagnostics.

The paper examines overfitting of model-selection criteria and the resulting optimistic performance estimates. Local relevance: hundreds of finite development comparisons and a reused150-cell cohort cannot substitute for an independent evaluation. This supports limiting new search and preserving the failed confirmation. It does not quantify the bias in these PUMA results or identify a preferred architecture.
