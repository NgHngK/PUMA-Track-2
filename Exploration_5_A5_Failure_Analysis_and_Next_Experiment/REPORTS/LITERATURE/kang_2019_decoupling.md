# Decoupling Representation and Classifier for Long-Tailed Recognition

Bingyi Kang, Saining Xie, Marcus Rohrbach, Zhicheng Yan, Albert Gordo, Jiashi Feng, Yannis Kalantidis.2019 preprint, ICLR2020.
Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09217
