usage: preselect_prompt6_dataset.py [-h] [--dataset-root DATASET_ROOT]
                                    [--historical-rois HISTORICAL_ROIS]
                                    [--out-dir OUT_DIR]
                                    [--group-csv GROUP_CSV] [--seed SEED]
                                    [--min-groups-per-split MIN_GROUPS_PER_SPLIT]

CPU-only preselection for PUMA Prompt 6. Builds the full GT manifest, audits
historical ROI overlap, preserves all unused ROIs as LOCKED_NATURAL, and
creates group-disjoint D300/D600/D900 + DEV + Prompt6 confirmation.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --dataset-root DATASET_ROOT
  --historical-rois HISTORICAL_ROIS
                        Authoritative CSV/TXT/JSON historical ROI artifact;
                        CSV requires roi column.
  --out-dir OUT_DIR
  --group-csv GROUP_CSV
                        Optional roi→patient/case/slide/group CSV. Without it,
                        grouping is ROI-level.
  --seed SEED
  --min-groups-per-split MIN_GROUPS_PER_SPLIT
