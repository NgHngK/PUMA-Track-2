# Exploration 1 — baseline audit and UNI2-head study

Exploration 1 established the provenance baseline for ten-class nuclei classification, audited the supplied class ratios and local 205-ROI census, verified the correct UNI2-h checkpoint contract, and compared a frozen single-crop UNI2-h linear head with early CNN/LoRA controls. The empirical work is development-only and GT-centred; it does not claim end-to-end Stage-1 performance or external validation.

Start with `REPORT_EXPLORATION_1.md`. The complete retained epoch record is `COMPLETE_EPOCH_LEDGER.md`. Metrics and predictions are under `RESULTS`; analysis, training, evaluation, tests, and configurations are separated under `CODE`; the retained visual diagnostic is under `QC`.
